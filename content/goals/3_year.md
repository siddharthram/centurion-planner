# 3-Year Goals

**Time Horizon**: {{year}} → 3 Years From Now

This document defines where you want to be in three years — professionally, personally, financially, and holistically.

Three years is long enough to make meaningful progress but short enough to feel real.

Update this annually. Review it quarterly to ensure your 1-year goals are aligned.

---

## Instructions

Your 3-year goals should be:
- **Directional**: They point you toward a specific future, even if the details are fuzzy
- **Ambitious**: They should require significant effort and growth
- **Vivid**: You should be able to imagine what life looks like when you achieve them
- **Aligned**: They should connect to your 10-year vision and values

Use your Vivid Vision (in `frameworks/vivid_vision.md`) to write a narrative version of these goals.

---

## The Big Question

**It's [DATE 3 YEARS FROM NOW]. What has changed in your life?**

Take a moment and imagine your life three years from now. Write a short narrative (1-3 paragraphs) describing:
- What you're working on
- Where you live
- Who you're with
- How you feel
- What you've accomplished
