# 10-Year Goals

**Time Horizon**: {{year}} → 10 Years From Now

This document defines where you want to be in 10 years — not as a precise roadmap, but as a directional vision.

Ten years is long enough to do something truly meaningful. It's also short enough that you'll actually live it.

Update this every 2-3 years, or whenever your vision fundamentally shifts.

---

## Instructions

Your 10-year goals should be:
- **Aspirational**: Push beyond what feels comfortable or "realistic"
- **Holistic**: Consider all dimensions of life, not just career
- **Honest**: What do you actually want, not what you think you should want?
- **Flexible**: This is a direction, not a contract. You're allowed to change your mind.

The purpose of this exercise is to give your life direction. If you don't know where you're going, any path will do.

---

## The Big Question

**It's [DATE 10 YEARS FROM NOW]. You've lived the best possible version of the last decade. What does your life look like?**

Write a narrative (2-5 paragraphs) describing your life in 10 years. Be specific. Use sensory details. Write in present tense.

Consider:
- What do you do with your days?
- Where do you live?
- Who are you with?
- What have you built, created, or contributed?
- How do you feel?
- What does your financial situation enable?
- What are you known for?
- What do people say about you?
