# 1-Year Goals

**Time Horizon**: {{date}} → {{year}} (Next 12 Months)

This document defines what you want to accomplish, experience, and become over the next 12 months.

Update this annually. Review it quarterly to track progress and course-correct.

---

## Instructions

Your 1-year goals should be:
- **Specific**: Clear enough that you'll know if you hit them
- **Meaningful**: They should matter to you, not just sound impressive
- **Achievable**: Ambitious but within reach
- **Aligned**: They should connect to your 3-year and 10-year vision

Use the Life Map framework to ensure you're not over-indexing on one dimension (e.g., all career, no health or relationships).
