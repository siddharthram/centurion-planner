# Annual Review Framework

**Adapted from Dr. Anthony Gustin's approach to honest, pattern-finding reflection.**

---

## What This Framework Does

The Annual Review is a structured look back at the past 12 months designed to:
- Identify what worked and what didn't
- Recognize patterns you can't see in daily life
- Extract lessons that apply to the next year
- Celebrate wins you've already forgotten
- Acknowledge failures without shame
- Decide what to stop, start, or continue

This is not a performance review. There's no grade. It's a mirror.

---

## When to Use It

Once a year. Block half a day. No meetings. No interruptions.

Pick a consistent time:
- End of December
- Your birthday
- Your company's founding anniversary
- Beginning of a new fiscal year

---

## How It Works

The Gustin-style annual review is question-based. You answer prompts honestly and look for patterns across your answers.

The review is divided into sections:

### 1. Highlights
What were the peak moments, wins, and breakthroughs?

### 2. Lowlights
What drained you, failed, or hurt?

### 3. Lessons
What did you learn that you'll carry forward?

### 4. Patterns
What keeps showing up year after year?

### 5. Decisions
What will you stop, start, or continue?

---

## Why This Framework Works

1. **It's honest, not aspirational**: You reflect on what actually happened, not what you wish had happened.
2. **It reveals blind spots**: Patterns emerge when you write them down.
3. **It prevents repeated mistakes**: If you keep setting the same goal and never hitting it, that's data.
4. **It builds self-knowledge**: You learn how you operate, what drains you, and what energizes you.

---

## Common Mistakes

- **Skipping the hard questions**: If a question makes you uncomfortable, that's exactly the one to answer.
- **Writing what sounds good**: No one else will read this. Be honest.
- **Focusing only on work**: Your life is not just your company.
- **Beating yourself up**: The point is learning, not self-punishment.

---

## Template Questions (Gustin-Style)

These questions are integrated into `reviews/annual/_template.md`. You can modify them to suit your needs.

**Highlights**:
- What were the top 3-5 wins this year?
- What are you most proud of that no one else sees?
- What brought you the most joy?

**Lowlights**:
- What were the biggest failures or disappointments?
- What drained your energy the most?
- What would you not repeat under any circumstances?

**Lessons**:
- What did you learn about yourself?
- What did you learn about leadership, business, or life?
- What assumptions were wrong?

**Patterns**:
- What keeps showing up in your reviews year after year?
- What goals do you keep setting but never achieve?
- Where do you consistently avoid hard decisions?

**Decisions**:
- What will you stop doing?
- What will you start doing?
- What will you continue doing?

---

## Output

After completing the annual review, you should have:
- A written record of the year (so you don't forget)
- 3-5 clear lessons
- A short list of patterns to address
- Decisions about what to change

Store this in `reviews/annual/[YEAR].md`.

Reference it during your next quarterly review to see if you're following through.
