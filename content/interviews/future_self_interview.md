# Future Self Interview

This is a guided self-interview where you imagine your future self — 5 years from now — and interview them about their life.

The purpose is to clarify what you actually want, not what you think you should want.

Set aside 45-60 minutes. Write in first person, present tense, as if you ARE your future self answering these questions.

---

## Setup

**Today's date**: [DATE]

**Future date**: [5 YEARS FROM NOW]

**Your age now**: [AGE]

**Your age then**: [AGE + 5]

---

## Instructions

Imagine it's 5 years from now. You've lived the best possible version of the next five years. Everything went as well as it reasonably could.

Now, answer these questions **as your future self**, in present tense, as if it's already true.

---

## Part 1: Daily Life

**It's [DATE 5 YEARS FROM NOW]. Describe a typical Tuesday.**

Where do you wake up? What's the first thing you do? What does your day look like from start to finish?

---

**How do you feel on a typical day?**

Energized? Calm? Stressed? Creative? Grounded? Describe your baseline emotional state.

---

**What does your morning routine look like?**

Be specific. What time do you wake up? What do you do before work?

---

**What does your evening look like?**

How do you end the day? Who are you with? What do you do to unwind?

---

## Part 2: Work and Career

**What are you working on?**

Are you still running [YOUR COMPANY]? Did you exit? Start something new? What's your primary focus?

---

**What does a typical workday look like?**

How many hours are you working? What are you spending your time on? What's your role?

---

**Who's on your team?**

Do you have a COO, CFO, or other key leaders? Are you still deeply involved, or have you stepped back?

---

**What are you known for professionally?**

What's your reputation in your industry or field?

---

**What's your relationship with work?**

Do you love it? Tolerate it? Is it a means to an end? Is it part of your identity?

---

## Part 3: Relationships

**Who are the most important people in your life?**

Family, partner, close friends — who matters most?

---

**How much time do you spend with them?**

Are you present, or are you distracted? What does quality time look like?

---

**Describe your closest relationship (partner, spouse, or closest friend).**

What's it like? How has it evolved over the past five years?

---

**If you have kids, what's your relationship with them like?**

Are you present? Absent? What role do you play in their lives?

---

**Who did you let go of in the past five years?**

Were there relationships you outgrew or intentionally ended?

---

## Part 4: Health and Energy

**How's your physical health?**

Are you stronger, weaker, or the same as five years ago? What are your habits?

---

**How's your mental and emotional health?**

Are you calm, anxious, fulfilled, burned out? What's your baseline?

---

**What does your fitness routine look like?**

Do you work out? How often? What do you do?

---

**How much do you sleep?**

How many hours per night? How's the quality?

---

**What do you eat?**

Are you intentional about nutrition, or do you not think about it much?

---

## Part 5: Finances and Lifestyle

**What's your financial situation?**

Are you wealthy? Comfortable? Still building? What's your net worth or annual income?

---

**Where do you live?**

Same place as now, or did you move? Describe your home.

---

**How do you spend your money?**

What do you prioritize financially? What do you splurge on? What do you not care about?

---

**Do you feel financial stress, or do you feel secure?**

Is money a concern, or is it handled?

---

**What does your wealth enable?**

What does financial security allow you to do that you couldn't do before?

---

## Part 6: Meaning and Purpose

**What gives your life meaning?**

Work? Family? Contribution? Creativity? What makes you feel like your life matters?

---

**What are you most proud of from the past five years?**

What did you build, create, or contribute that makes you feel good?

---

**What impact have you had?**

On your company, your industry, your family, or the world — what's different because you existed?

---

**What are you building that will outlast you?**

Legacy, impact, systems, relationships — what will remain after you're gone?

---

## Part 7: Fun and Joy

**What do you do for fun?**

Hobbies, play, experiences — what brings you joy?

---

**When was the last time you laughed hard?**

What made you laugh?

---

**What's something you do regularly that has nothing to do with work or productivity?**

What do you do just because you enjoy it?

---

**What's the most fun you've had in the past year?**

Describe a specific experience or moment.

---

## Part 8: Reflection

**What's the biggest change you made in the past five years?**

What decision, shift, or action had the most impact?

---

**What did you stop doing?**

What did you let go of or walk away from?

---

**What surprised you about the past five years?**

What turned out differently than you expected?

---

**If you could go back and talk to yourself five years ago (which is you, today), what would you say?**

What advice, encouragement, or warning would you give?

---

## Part 9: Challenges and Growth

**What was the hardest part of the past five years?**

What did you struggle with? What hurt?

---

**How did you grow as a person?**

What's different about who you are now compared to five years ago?

---

**What did you learn that you wish you'd known sooner?**

What lessons would have saved you time, pain, or energy?

---

**What are you still working on?**

What's unfinished or ongoing?

---

## Part 10: Synthesis

**In one paragraph, describe your life five years from now.**

Capture the essence of your future life in a short narrative.

---

**How does this future self feel about the past five years?**

Satisfied? Proud? Regretful? Grateful?

---

**What's the one thing you did in the past five years that made all the difference?**

If you could point to a single decision, action, or shift that changed everything, what was it?

---

## Returning to the Present

Now, step out of your future self and return to the present.

**What did you learn from this exercise?**

What insights or realizations came up?

---

**What's the gap between your future self and your current self?**

What needs to change for that future to become real?

---

**What's one decision you need to make now to become that future self?**

What's the first step?

---

**What are you willing to sacrifice or let go of to get there?**

What will you need to stop, delegate, or release?

---

**What's one commitment you're making to your future self?**

What will you start doing today?

**Purpose**: This interview is designed to clarify your long-term vision by stepping into the future and imagining it as if it's already real. Complete this once, then revisit every 1-2 years to see if your vision has changed. Use your answers to inform your Vivid Vision, 3-year goals, and major life decisions.
