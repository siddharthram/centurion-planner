# Past Year Reflection Interview

This is a guided self-interview designed to help you reflect on the past 12 months with honesty and clarity.

Set aside 60-90 minutes. Answer each question in writing. Don't overthink — write what comes to mind first, then refine if needed.

No one else will read this. Be honest.

---

## Part 1: Highlights

**Tell me about the last year. Start with the highlights.**

What were the peak moments? What are you most proud of?

---

**What went better than expected?**

Where did you exceed your own expectations? What surprised you on the upside?

---

**What are you proud of that no one else sees?**

What did you do quietly, without recognition, that you're still proud of?

---

**What brought you the most joy this year?**

Not accomplishments — joy. What moments made you feel alive, happy, or fulfilled?

---

**Who made the biggest positive impact on your life this year?**

Who helped you, challenged you, or supported you in ways that mattered?

---

## Part 2: Lowlights

**Now the hard part. What were the biggest disappointments or failures this year?**

Where did things not go as planned? What hurt?

---

**What drained your energy the most?**

What situations, people, or responsibilities left you feeling depleted?

---

**Where did you avoid hard decisions?**

What did you know you should address but didn't? What did you delay or ignore?

---

**What would you not repeat under any circumstances?**

If you could go back, what would you skip entirely?

---

**What patterns do you notice in your struggles?**

Do the same issues keep showing up? What's the common thread?

---

## Part 3: Lessons and Growth

**What did you learn about yourself this year?**

What did you discover about how you operate, what you value, or what you need?

---

**What did you learn about leadership, business, or life?**

What assumptions were wrong? What new mental models did you adopt?

---

**If you could give advice to yourself at the beginning of this year, what would you say?**

Knowing what you know now, what would you have done differently?

---

**What became clearer this year?**

What do you now understand that was fuzzy before?

---

## Part 4: Relationships

**How did your most important relationships change this year?**

Family, partnership, close friendships — what improved? What deteriorated?

---

**Where were you present, and where were you absent?**

When did you show up fully? When did you phone it in?

---

**Who did you lose touch with that you wish you hadn't?**

Is there anyone you want to reconnect with?

---

**Who did you spend time with that you shouldn't have?**

Were there relationships that drained you or pulled you off course?

---

## Part 5: Work and Career

**What was your biggest professional win this year?**

Not revenue or valuation — what are you most proud of in your work?

---

**What was your biggest professional mistake or missed opportunity?**

Where did you misread the situation, make the wrong call, or let something slip?

---

**Did you grow as a leader this year?**

How are you different as a leader now compared to 12 months ago?

---

**What part of your role energizes you, and what part drains you?**

If you could only do one part of your job, what would it be? What would you delegate or eliminate?

---

**If this year repeated ten times, would you be satisfied?**

Is your current trajectory one you want to continue, or does something need to change?

---

## Part 6: Health and Energy

**How was your physical health this year?**

Fitness, sleep, energy, nutrition — better or worse than last year?

---

**How was your mental and emotional health?**

Stress, anxiety, mood, resilience — where were you strong, and where were you struggling?

---

**What habits did you build or break this year?**

What new routines stuck? What old patterns did you finally let go of?

---

**When did you feel most energized this year?**

What activities, environments, or people gave you energy?

---

**When did you feel most depleted?**

What consistently drained you?

---

## Part 7: Meaning and Purpose

**Did you feel a sense of purpose this year?**

Did your work and life feel meaningful, or did it feel like going through the motions?

---

**What are you building that will outlast you?**

Legacy, impact, contribution — what are you creating that matters beyond yourself?

---

**If you died today, what would you regret not doing?**

This is blunt, but important. What's unfinished that truly matters to you?

---

**What do you want to be known for?**

Not by the world — by the people who know you best. What do you want them to say about you?

---
