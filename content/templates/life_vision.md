# Review

---

## Alignment Check

Before you finish, answer honestly:

**Is this vision authentically yours?** Or are you chasing someone else's definition of success?

**Is this vision balanced?** Or are you sacrificing relationships, health, or meaning for career and wealth?

**If you achieve this vision, will you feel fulfilled?** Or will you immediately move the goalposts?

---

## Review Schedule

- **Quarterly**: Skim this document. Are you on track?
- **Annually**: Deep review. Update 3-year milestones and priorities.
- **Every 2-3 years**: Revisit 10-year vision. Has it evolved?
