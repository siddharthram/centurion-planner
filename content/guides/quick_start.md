# Quick Start Guide

**You have 15 minutes. Here's how to get started.**

---

## Step 1: Personalize Your North Star (5 minutes)

Open `north_star.md` and fill in the placeholders:

- Your role
- Your company
- Your stage of life
- Your top 5 priorities right now

Don't overthink it. Write what's true today.

---

## Step 2: Do Today's Daily Check-In (5 minutes)

1. Create a new file: `reviews/daily/[TODAY'S DATE].md`
   - Format: `YYYY-MM-DD.md` (e.g., `2026-01-04.md`)

2. Copy the template from `reviews/daily/_template.md`

3. Answer the 5 questions:
   - Energy level (1-10)
   - One meaningful win
   - One friction point
   - One thing to let go of
   - One priority for tomorrow

4. Save it.

Done. You're using the system.

---

## Step 3: Read the Principles (5 minutes)

Open `principles.md` and read through the operating principles.

Edit any that don't resonate. Add your own at the bottom.

These principles will guide how you use the system and make decisions.

---

## That's It.

You've personalized the system and started using it.

**Tomorrow**: Do another daily check-in.

**This weekend**: Do your first weekly review using `reviews/weekly/_template.md`.

**In 3 months**: Do your first quarterly review.

**In 12 months**: Do your first annual review.

The system gets more valuable the longer you use it.

---

## Optional Next Steps (When You Have More Time)

- Complete `interviews/identity_and_values.md` (60 minutes)
- Fill out your 1-year goals in `goals/1_year.md` (30 minutes)
- Read through the framework explanations in `frameworks/` (20 minutes)
- Upload past annual reviews or notes to `uploads/` and summarize them in `memory.md` (60 minutes)

But you don't need to do any of this right now.

Just do a daily check-in today. That's enough.

---

**The system works when you use it. Start small. Build from there.**
